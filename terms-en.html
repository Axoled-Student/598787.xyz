Purpose

NTU Gmail Sync is a personal utility that allows users to authorize access to their own Google account for Gmail synchronization functionality.

User authorization

Users must authorize Google access through Google's OAuth consent process. Users may revoke that access at any time from their Google Account settings.

Acceptable use

Users may use the application only with accounts and data they are authorized to access and in compliance with applicable law and Google's terms and policies.

Availability

The application is provided without a guarantee of uninterrupted availability. Features may be changed or discontinued as the project evolves.

Privacy
