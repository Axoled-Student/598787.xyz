01 / About

Small in scope.
Clear in purpose.

Axo Mail is an early-stage software initiative focused on email utilities and related developer tools.

Our approach is straightforward: start with a concrete problem, build something useful, document what it does, and improve it over time.

The project is currently independently developed. We publish information about existing work and describe future features as plans, rather than presenting prototypes as finished products.

02 / Projects

What we're working on

Our existing Gmail utility and the directions we're researching next.

Featured project / 01

NTU Gmail Sync

Existing project

NTU Gmail Sync is a personal utility intended to let a user authorize their own Google account and perform Gmail synchronization for their requested workflow.

The project uses Google OAuth as its authorization mechanism. This website documents its purpose and links to the relevant data-handling policies; the website itself is informational and does not connect to your Gmail account.

04 / Information

Frequently asked questions

Basic information about the project and how it is being developed.

Is Axo Mail a registered company?

Axo Mail is described here as an independently developed software project. This website does not claim legal incorporation, investment, or company registration.

Can I use Claude-powered email features today?

No. Claude-powered features are an area of development and research. This site does not offer a live AI inbox or a production Claude integration.

Does this website access my Gmail account?

No. This is a public information website. NTU Gmail Sync is a separate utility; any Gmail authorization must be initiated by the user through Google's OAuth process.

Is the project affiliated with Google, NTU, or Anthropic?

No. Axo Mail and NTU Gmail Sync are independent projects and are not official services of Google, National Taiwan University, or Anthropic.
