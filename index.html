Existing project

NTU Gmail Sync

An independently developed personal utility intended to connect a user's own Google account and synchronize Gmail data for a user-requested workflow.

  • Google OAuth-based account authorization
  • Gmail synchronization within the permissions granted by the user
  • Published information, privacy policy, and terms

About Axo Mail

Small project. Deliberate direction.

Axo Mail is the name for an independent, early-stage email tooling initiative. We're interested in practical software that respects user choice and makes information easier to use.
